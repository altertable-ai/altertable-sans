What this proof does and does not contain

374 encoded characters, tabular default numerals, and an initial kerning pass. Text has heavier strokes and more spacing than Display. The family has six Text weights with italics and a separate Regular Display. These are static fonts, not a finished optical-size system.

Variable axes, complete language coverage, ligatures, and hinting are still to come. The outlines use flattened stroke geometry and need further optical correction and curve editing before release.
