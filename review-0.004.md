# Altertable Sans 0.004 review

Compared against the locally loaded Anthropic Sans webfont: weight 400,
Text optical size 16, each letter at 48 px and 14 px. Word proofs use 24 px;
the g proof also covers 12, 16, 20, and 28 px. Display is compared separately
at 48 px with the reference optical size set to 48. Source outlines remain
independently drawn; the build does not read the reference font.

Each row records a visual review, not a claim of exact matching. Changes are
relative to the preserved 0.003 files. Unchanged characters were compared too.

All ten numerals were re-rendered and compared. Their 0.003 drawings and tabular
advances are retained in this pass. The 3/5/8 still look more rounded than the
reference; no changes were forced merely to make every row different.

The full comparison includes punctuation and accented spacing samples. There
are 107 seed pairs and 1,267 expanded pairs. Seed-pair outline collision checks
are geometric checks only: they do not replace inspection of rasterized text.
The fonts remain unhinted prototypes with flattened outlines.
